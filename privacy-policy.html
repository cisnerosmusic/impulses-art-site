Privacy & Confidentiality Policy

Our commitment to protecting the privacy and dignity of every participant.

Confidentiality of Sessions

All information shared during IMPULSES.ART music therapy sessions is treated as strictly confidential. We do not record, share, or disclose any personal information, emotional expressions, or therapeutic content without the explicit written consent of the participant or their legal guardian.

This commitment applies to all session formats: individual, group, virtual, and community workshops.

Data Collection

IMPULSES.ART collects only the minimum information necessary to provide our services: name, contact information, and any relevant background you choose to share during intake. We do not collect financial data, government identification numbers, or immigration status information.

Our website uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service that does not use cookies, does not collect personal data, and is fully compliant with GDPR and CCPA.

Safe Space Commitment

We recognize that many of our participants come from vulnerable situations, including displacement, trauma, and cultural transition. IMPULSES.ART is committed to providing a safe, non-judgmental environment where every person is treated with dignity and respect regardless of their immigration status, nationality, language, gender, religion, or background.

No information from our sessions will ever be shared with immigration authorities, employers, or any third party without explicit consent, except where required by law to prevent imminent harm.
