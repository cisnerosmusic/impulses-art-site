Professional Music Therapy Project

IMPULSES.ART is a professional music therapy project that combines live piano performance with scientific and psychological approaches to healing. Founded and directed by Ernesto Cisneros Cino, an internationally experienced pianist, composer, and educator, the project focuses on supporting immigrant and refugee communities.

IMPULSES.ART creates a safe space for emotional expression, trauma recovery, and cultural integration. Through live music, research-based methods, and artistic creativity, the initiative helps individuals and groups find resilience, hope, and meaningful connection.

The music used in our sessions, composed and performed by Ernesto Cisneros Cino, is available for listening at ernestocisneros.art and for purchase at impulses.online/music.

Areas of Expertise

Music Therapy, Emotional Expression, Trauma Recovery, Cultural Integration

Our music therapy services are specifically designed to meet the emotional and psychological needs of immigrant and refugee communities. We combine live piano performance, expressive arts, and evidence-based therapeutic techniques to support individuals and groups on their healing journey.

At IMPULSES.ART, we are committed to creating a safe and nurturing environment where clients can explore their emotions, process trauma, and build resilience. Through music, we foster cultural integration and strengthen the human connection that helps communities thrive.
