Our Services

Tailored music therapy for immigrant and refugee communities, designed with cultural sensitivity and scientific rigor.

Group Sessions

Our group sessions foster shared healing, social cohesion, and community bonds through live piano improvisation and participatory dynamics. These sessions are particularly powerful for communities with collective cultural backgrounds, creating spaces where participants feel truly seen and heard.

Individual Sessions

One-on-one sessions provide personalized, deeply focused therapeutic work tailored to your unique needs. With real-time adaptation and individualized attention, these sessions allow for profound personal healing and transformation at your own pace.

Community Workshops

We design and facilitate specialized workshops for organizations, educational institutions, churches, and cultural centers. These sessions can address specific community needs—from trauma-informed healing to cultural integration—and serve as powerful catalysts for collective wellness.

Virtual Sessions

Our virtual sessions maintain the full quality and interactivity of live music therapy, bringing healing into your home. Live piano connection and real-time engagement ensure that distance is no barrier to transformative therapeutic experience.
